About May Vision Multimedia

关于我们

We are a Multimedia Production House

我们是一家多媒体制作公司

We aim to provide 1 stop multimedia production services to corporate company, event agency and organizations.

我们致力于为企业公司、活动策划公司及机构提供一站式多媒体制作服务。

Based in Kuala Lumpur, May Vision Multimedia is a FINAS & KKMM licensed production house. From 2016 we have produced more than 80 episodes of TV programmes for RTM TV1, TV2, Berita RTM and TV Okey, and co-produced programmes for China CCTV.
