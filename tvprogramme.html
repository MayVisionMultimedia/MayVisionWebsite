Tech Today is a 13-episode English programme (2022-2023) that explores how technology is changing the way we live, work, learn and manage money. Produced by May Vision Multimedia Studio.

My Health, My Wealth is a 13-episode English magazine programme on RTM TV Okey that looks at health as the foundation of wealth. Each episode explores one topic with doctors, specialists and practitioners, from eye care with Orthokeratology, the myth of coconut oil, the Wim Hof Method, intermittent fasting and mental health, to fun runs and marathons, anti aging and stem cell technology, the 3 pillars in healthcare, the IOT and tokenized healthcare ecosystem, and social wellbeing. Produced by May Vision Multimedia Studio.

My Health, My Wealth 是一档在 RTM TV Okey 播出、共13集的英语杂志式节目，探讨健康是财富的根基。每集邀请医生、专家与实践者探讨一个主题，内容包括角膜塑形、椰子油的迷思、冰人呼吸法、间歇性断食、心理健康、趣味跑与马拉松、抗衰老与干细胞技术、医疗保健三大支柱，物联网与代币化医疗生态系统，以及社交健康。由 May Vision Multimedia Studio 制作。
