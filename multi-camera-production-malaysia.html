Multi Camera Production

Multi Camera Production (MCP) in Malaysia

Multi camera production (MCP) uses several cameras covering an event at once, switched live, so you get the stage, the speaker and the audience in one polished programme. We run MCP for events across Malaysia from our base in Kuala Lumpur.

Where MCP is used

  • Conferences, forums and seminars
  • Product and event launches
  • Award nights and annual dinners
  • Concerts and large stage events

What you get

  • MCP livefeed projected to the event hall LED screen
  • Livestreaming to Facebook, YouTube, Zoom or Microsoft Teams
  • Full event recording and a highlight video

Related

See video livefeed and livestreaming.

How an MCP crew works

Several camera operators cover different angles at once, such as a wide shot, close-ups of speakers and the audience. A director switches between the cameras live, so the feed that reaches the screen or the stream is presented nicely.

When to choose MCP

Choose MCP when your event has a stage and a larger audience, when you want a livefeed on the hall screens, or when you want a polished livestream. For smaller sessions, single camera production may be enough, and we will tell you honestly which one fits.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between MCP and SCP?

SCP is single camera production. MCP uses multiple cameras with live switching, which suits stage events and livefeed to a big screen.

What does MCP stand for?

MCP stands for Multi Camera Production.

Can you record and livestream with MCP at the same time?

Yes. We can livestream while recording the full event.
