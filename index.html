About Us

One Stop Production House

May Vision Multimedia is a full-service creative multimedia production house based in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. We specialise in high-quality visual content for corporations, government bodies, organisations, and SMEs across the country.

From 2016 to present, we have produced over 80 episodes of TV programmes for RTM TV1, TV2, TV5 & TV Okey, and co-produced 26 episodes for China CCTV (Nanyang Trip Seasons 1 & 2). We are a FINAS & KKMM licensed production house.

We were also among the first in Malaysia to offer complete virtual event solutions during MCO in March 2020, serving clients from Malaysia, Singapore, Brunei, and the USA.
