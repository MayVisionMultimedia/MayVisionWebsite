Videography

Videography Services in Malaysia

May Vision Multimedia Studio is a Kuala Lumpur video production house providing professional videography services across Malaysia. From a single-camera shoot to a full multi-camera production, our team handles everything in-house — planning, filming, editing and delivery.

What our videography service covers

  • Corporate video, product video and promotional video
  • Event videography — annual dinners, product launches, conferences, forums, seminars, team-building, exhibitions, roadshows and concerts
  • TV production, podcast production, documentary and TV content
  • Livefeed & livestreaming, from single camera (SCP) to multi camera (MCP)
  • VHS tape conversion to digital, DVD/CD duplication

From idea to final video

Every project follows three stages: pre-production (idea development, production planning, script writing, voice over recording), production (shooting, directing, camera, lighting and sound equipment, production management) and post-production (video editing and motion graphics).
