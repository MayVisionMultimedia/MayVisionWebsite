Event Videography

Event Videography in Kuala Lumpur & Malaysia

We provide one stop event videography for any kind of event — indoor or outdoor, small or large — with full recording, livefeed, livestream and cinematic highlight video.

Event videography services

  • Full event video recording
  • MCP livefeed to the event hall LED screen
  • Event livestreaming to Facebook or YouTube
  • Event highlight (recap) video
  • Speaker and guest interviews

Events we cover

Event and product launches, conference and forum, annual dinners, international events, outdoor and marathon events, commercial and direct sales award nights, team-building, company award events, roadshows, concerts, competitions and festivals. Watch samples on our event video page.
